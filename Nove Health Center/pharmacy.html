Sidee baan u dalban karaa daawo leh warqad dhakhtar?

Waa sahlan tahay. Kaliya sawir warqadda dhakhtarka (Prescription), ka dibna ku geli portal-kayaga sugan ee "Access Secure Ordering". Farmashiisyaashayada ayaa xaqiijin doona ka dibna daawada ayaa laguugu keenayaa gurigaaga.

Ma bixisaan adeegga keenista 24 saac (Emergency)?

Haa, Farmashiyaha Nova wuxuu bixiyaa Express Delivery oo shaqaysa 24 saac. Daawooyinka degdegga ah waxaan ku keenaa 30-45 daqiiqo gudahood Muqdisho.

Sidee baan u xaqiijin karaa tayada daawada?

Nova Health waxay isticmaashaa nidaamka Smart Verification. Baakad kasta waxay leedahay QR-Code aad ka sawiri kartid taleefankaaga si aad u hubiso taariikhda soo saarista iyo rasmiga-nimada.

Qaabkee baan bixiyay lacagta?

Waxaan aqbalnaa dhammaan hababka lacag bixinta ee caanka ah sida EVC Plus, e-Dahab, Zaad, iyo Credit Cards. Lacagta waxaa lagu bixiyaa onlayn adigoo isticmaalaya checkout-kayaga adag.

Ma u baahan tahay caawinaad dheeri ah?

Khubaradayada farmashiyaha ayaa hadda onlayn u ah inay ku caawiyaan.

Haa, waxaan leenahay qeyb u gaar ah degdegga oo shaqaysa 24 saac. Haddii ay jirto baahi degdeg ah, fadlan isticmaal taleefanka gargaarka degdegga ah ee ku yaal websaydka.
